# Street CEO — Accounting Rules (v0, 2026-10-29)

Status: initial principles. Detailed policy memos live in `docs/finance/`
(Ruzivo, not yet written). Implementer: Verengai under Ruzivo's review.

## Binding principles

1. Every money movement is balanced, dated, and linked to a source
   (sale, purchase, payment, expense, adjustment).
2. Corrections happen through reversal entries, never silent edits or deletes.
3. Cash and bank reconciliations surface discrepancies with explanations;
   nothing auto-hides.
4. Cost of sales links to Dura's stock movements so margins and P&L agree.
5. Reports (P&L, cashflow) reproduce from ledger queries; no spreadsheet-side numbers.
6. No backdating except through an explicit, approved reversal policy.
