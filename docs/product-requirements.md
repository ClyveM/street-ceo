# Street CEO — current demo requirements

Updated 2026-11-02. The October 29 draft is superseded by the Owner-approved
connected Android scope. The authoritative builder brief is
[Product documentation and demo acceptance](product-demo-builder.md).

Required: catalogue, manual and hands-free Apo sales, Cash/Bank tender and cash
change, stock, sales history, local SQLite durability, starter unit costs for
future COGS, and sanitized persistent request/response investigations.

Offline sync, PostgreSQL deployment, the separate team-view web app, verified
bank settlement, full ledger reconciliation and public production deployment
are outside this demo. See [acceptance](demo-mvp-acceptance.md) and the latest
[verification](demo-mvp-verification.md) for implemented behavior and device limits.
